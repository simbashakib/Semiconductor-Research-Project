# Entegris–TSMC relationship

Report review: 18 November 2026. This is an economic screening note, not evidence
of return predictability.

## Product and economic role

TSMC names Entegris in both the **chemicals** and **gases** categories of its
raw-materials supply table. These inputs enter wafer fabrication. The table
describes specifications, quality reviews, supplier qualification and supply-risk
management, but does not identify the particular Entegris products TSMC buys.

The economic mechanisms to investigate are production demand, input availability,
quality and yield. Higher fab output could increase materials demand; a material
shortage or quality problem could constrain output or raise costs. These are
plausible mechanisms, not measured effects. Neither Entegris' dependence on TSMC
nor TSMC's expenditure on Entegris is established by these disclosures.

The named category listings support Entegris as an economically relevant
candidate. They do not by themselves prove continuous bilateral purchasing
throughout 2010–2022. The large procurement counterparties are anonymised:
**do not assign Entegris to an anonymous supplier or infer its purchasing share**.

## Possible information transmission

These are proposed explanations to test, not findings:

- **Demand information:** TSMC's order or production news could inform materials
  demand; supplier news might reveal broader fab activity. Either direction is
  possible.
- **Supply information:** a materials shortage could benefit a supplier's pricing
  while hurting fab output, so even the expected return sign is conditional.
- **Common influences:** market risk, the semiconductor cycle and shared end
  demand could move both stocks without transmission between them.

Public announcements and market closes provide observable timestamps. An annual
report is available from its release, not its fiscal year-end. If daily returns
are used instead of report variables, define the return intervals explicitly.
Operational lead times do not establish delayed stock-price adjustment.

Evidence against a useful leading signal would include unstable signs, no
improvement over market/sector and own-history baselines, or effects that vanish
under chronological validation. Same-day correlation alone is insufficient.
